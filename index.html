A practical approach

Clear scope. Safer work. Useful records.

Operational sites need more than a service list. They need an understandable scope, appropriate controls, timely communication and documentation that helps teams make informed decisions.

  • Understand the asset, site conditions and desired outcome before work begins.
  • Align the proposed scope with relevant safety and compliance requirements.
  • Keep communication and findings clear for the people responsible for the site.
  • Consider preventative actions as well as immediate repairs or response.
