Questions clients often ask

Start with the essentials.

What information should I include in an enquiry?

Share your organisation, site location, asset or system involved, the issue or objective, any relevant deadline and the best way to contact you.

Can Alpine support every service listed on this site?

The pages describe areas to discuss and common service categories. Specific delivery capabilities, qualifications, availability and partner requirements must be confirmed directly before engagement.

Do you cover locations outside Nairobi?

Please share the project location. Geographic coverage and mobilisation arrangements should be confirmed for each requirement.

Can I request a site assessment or proposal?

Use the contact form to describe the site, the outcome you need and the timing. The team can then clarify whether the requirement is within scope.
