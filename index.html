Frequently asked questions

Start with the essentials.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Share your organisation, site location, asset or system, the issue or objective and any deadline.

Can Alpine provide every service listed?

The pages describe service categories to discuss. Actual delivery capabilities, qualifications, availability and partner requirements must be confirmed before engagement.

Do you cover locations outside Nairobi?

Share the project location so geographic coverage and mobilisation can be confirmed.

Can I request an assessment or proposal?
