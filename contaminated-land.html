Service overview

Site history and investigation

A contaminated-land requirement may need review of historical land use, a conceptual site model, investigation planning, laboratory analysis, risk assessment and remediation design by qualified specialists.

What to prepare

Site history, previous environmental reports, proposed land use, available plans, known incidents and relevant planning or regulatory requirements.

Possible next steps

A competent specialist can advise on investigation scope, sampling and analysis, risk evaluation, remediation and verification requirements. No conclusion about a site can be drawn from this page alone.
