Incident readiness

Prepare for the unexpected. Clarify the response.

Planning for environmental incidents starts with safe escalation, clear roles and the involvement of appropriately qualified response teams.

Discuss your requirement ↗Explore services
Preparedness first

Safety and escalation come before a website enquiry.

If an incident is happening now, follow the site's emergency procedures and contact local emergency services or a qualified response provider as appropriate. Do not wait for a response from this website.

For non-emergency planning, useful topics include contact trees, site access, spill and pollution risks, available plans, environmental receptors and the roles of trained personnel.

Planning checklist

Information worth having ready.

  • Site address and safe access details
  • Nature of the incident or scenario and when it occurred
  • Known materials, hazards and affected areas, where safe to identify
  • Site emergency plan, relevant safety data and contact tree
  • Known drains, waterways or sensitive receptors that may be affected
  • Incident lead, site owner and any responders already contacted
