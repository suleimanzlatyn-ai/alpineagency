Capability overview

Incident Readiness & Response

During a site incident, people need clear responsibilities, reliable information and a safe escalation route. This guidance does not replace emergency services or qualified responders.

Actual service scope, qualifications, availability and delivery arrangements should be confirmed directly before engagement. Where specialist work is needed, roles and responsibilities should be agreed before work begins.

01 / FOCUS AREA

Initial safety & escalation

Prioritise life safety, follow the site's emergency plan and contact emergency services when required.

02 / FOCUS AREA

Spill & pollution concerns

Identify the material only when safe, prevent exposure where trained to do so, protect drains only if safe and contact qualified responders.

03 / FOCUS AREA

Incident information

Record location, time, observed conditions, affected areas and actions taken without putting people at risk.

04 / FOCUS AREA

Readiness planning

Review contacts, responsibilities, access arrangements, relevant site plans, drills and specialist response agreements.
