What helps us assess coverage

A few details can prevent delays.

Include these points when asking about a service location.

01 / LOCATION

Site & access

Town or area, site type, access procedures, operating hours and any relevant travel or logistics constraints.

02 / URGENCY

Timing & priority

Preferred dates, deadlines and whether there is an immediate safety or environmental issue requiring a separate emergency route.

03 / TECHNICAL

Required expertise

Asset type, requested task, available reports and any standards, permits or specialist credentials involved.

04 / DELIVERY

Scope & coordination

Expected deliverables, number of sites, responsible contacts, reporting needs and third-party interfaces.
