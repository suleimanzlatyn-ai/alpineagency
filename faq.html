Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about discussing environmental, infrastructure and site-support requirements with Alpine Agencies.

General information only. This guide is not a substitute for site-specific assessment, emergency advice, engineering design or qualified environmental and legal guidance.

SECTION 01

What should I include in an enquiry?

Share your organisation, site location, the issue or objective, relevant deadlines, and any available photos or reports. Avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary.

SECTION 02

Does Alpine provide every specialist service directly?

Service availability, qualifications and delivery arrangements must be confirmed with Alpine before engagement. Some requirements may need qualified specialist partners.

SECTION 03

Can I request support outside Nairobi?

Share the location and scope through the contact page. Coverage, mobilisation and timing must be confirmed for each requirement.

SECTION 04

How quickly will I receive a response?

Timing depends on availability and the enquiry. For an urgent safety or environmental incident, contact the appropriate emergency services and competent response authorities first.

SECTION 05

Does the form save my enquiry online?

The current form prepares an email in your device's email app. It is not an online case-tracking system and does not confirm that an email was sent.
