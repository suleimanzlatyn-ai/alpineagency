FAQs

Good questions make better projects.

Use these answers as a starting point. Confirm current Alpine service scope, credentials, availability and pricing directly before commissioning work.

Discuss a project ↗Explore services
How do I know which service I need?

Start by describing the site, asset or issue, the outcome you need and any time or safety constraints. Alpine can then clarify whether the requirement fits its confirmed scope or needs specialist input.

Does Alpine provide emergency response?

Do not assume emergency coverage, response times or 24/7 availability from this website. Contact Alpine directly to verify arrangements. For an active emergency, follow site procedures and contact local emergency services.

Which parts of Kenya do you cover?

Geographic coverage and mobilisation should be confirmed for each project. Include the exact site location in your enquiry.

Can you work with specialist contractors?

Ask Alpine to confirm whether partners are needed, who would contract them, what credentials apply and who is responsible for quality and delivery.

How should I prepare for a first discussion?

Prepare the site location, a short description of the challenge, relevant photographs or records if available, the desired outcome and your preferred timeline.

Can I request a quotation?

Yes, use the contact page to describe the requirement. A reliable quotation may require a scope discussion, site information and clarification of any specialist work.

Are case studies on this website completed Alpine projects?

Only project stories explicitly identified and verified by Alpine should be treated as completed projects. The portfolio page includes templates and does not claim unverified case studies as fact.
