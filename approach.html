From first call to next step

Understand the need before defining the solution.

Use this process to prepare for an enquiry, site review or project scoping conversation.

PHASE 01

Discover

Clarify the site location, asset or environmental issue, timeline, operating constraints and desired outcome.

PHASE 02

Assess

Identify known risks, information gaps, applicable requirements and the specialists that may need to be involved.

PHASE 03

Plan

Define the proposed scope, responsibilities, access needs, communication and delivery sequence.

PHASE 04

Review

Confirm completion criteria, records, follow-up actions and future maintenance or resilience needs.

Good project preparation

Bring the details that help everyone make a better decision.

  • Site address and operating environment.
  • Photos, asset details or relevant reports, if available.
  • What has happened and what you have already tried.
  • Any immediate safety, environmental or operational concern.
  • Timing, access restrictions and key stakeholders.

Need help defining the scope?

A first conversation can help identify the right questions and whether specialist assessment is needed.
