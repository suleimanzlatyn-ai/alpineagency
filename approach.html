Four-stage approach

A process designed to reduce assumptions.

A structured first conversation can clarify the site, outcome, constraints, scope and specialist input needed before anyone commits to work.

01

Listen

Understand the location, operating context, issue, objective and urgency.

02

Assess

Review available information, constraints, risks and open questions.

03

Scope

Confirm deliverables, responsibilities, expertise, access and timelines.

04

Follow through

Agree reporting, acceptance criteria, records and next actions.

Good information helps

What makes a first conversation useful?

  • Specific site location and access arrangements
  • Clear statement of the challenge and desired outcome
  • Known risks, operating constraints and deadlines
  • Existing drawings, inspection records or reports
  • Procurement, budget or approval requirements if known
