A clear sequence

Four steps to a better-defined requirement.

The exact workflow depends on the project, but the goal is to reduce uncertainty before work is agreed.

STEP 01

Understand

Capture the site, asset, concern, urgency and intended outcome.

STEP 02

Assess

Identify information gaps, risks, access needs and specialist disciplines.

STEP 03

Define

Confirm deliverables, responsibilities, assumptions and constraints.

STEP 04

Review

Agree communication, documentation and appropriate follow-up.

Before work begins

Professional delivery is more than showing up.

For the project at hand, confirm relevant qualifications, insurance, permits, site induction, method statements, risk assessments, emergency arrangements and any client or statutory requirements.

  • Written scope and clear exclusions
  • Named responsibilities and communication route
  • Safe access and agreed operating windows
  • Deliverables and acceptance criteria
  • Escalation and change-control process
