HOW WE WORK

A clear process from first call to close-out.

Every site is different. A structured process helps make the scope safer, the work easier to coordinate and the outcome more useful.

Discuss your site ↗
01 / UNDERSTAND

Understand

Gather the location, asset details, operating conditions, immediate risks and desired outcome.

02 / ASSESS

Assess

Agree whether a site visit, inspection, testing or specialist input is needed to define the work.

03 / PLAN

Plan

Clarify scope, access, safety controls, schedule, deliverables, exclusions and commercial terms.

04 / EXECUTE

Execute

Carry out the agreed work with appropriate controls and communication with the site contact.

05 / DOCUMENT

Document

Record findings, work completed, limitations and outstanding risks or corrective actions.

06 / MAINTAIN

Maintain

Where needed, establish follow-up actions and a practical inspection or maintenance schedule.

What to prepare

  • Site location and site contact.
  • Asset type and service required.
  • Whether an active safety or environmental incident is underway.
  • Preferred timing, access rules and site requirements.
  • Relevant photos or inspection notes, if available.
