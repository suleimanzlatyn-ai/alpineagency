A considered process

Clear thinking. Defined next steps.

Every site is different. A structured conversation helps identify what is known, what needs checking and which next steps are appropriate.

01 / PROCESS

Understand

Clarify the site, the challenge, priorities, timing and desired outcome.

02 / PROCESS

Assess

Identify available information, risks, constraints and specialist requirements.

03 / PROCESS

Plan

Agree a practical scope, responsibilities, communication and next actions.

04 / PROCESS

Review

Capture outcomes, outstanding actions and opportunities to improve resilience.

Before a project begins

Ask the questions that reduce uncertainty.

A useful initial conversation should establish the location, the problem, its urgency, any immediate safety concerns, existing reports and the outcome you need.

  • What has happened or what needs to change?
  • Which site, asset or environmental pathway is involved?
  • Are there immediate hazards or time-sensitive requirements?
  • What evidence, plans, reports or inspection records already exist?
  • Who needs to approve the scope and next steps?
