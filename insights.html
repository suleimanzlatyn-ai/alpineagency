Practical guidance

Better prepared sites start with better questions.

Use these field-ready checklists to prepare a useful service enquiry and help your team discuss site requirements clearly.

⌖
Checklist 01

Preparing a site-service enquiry

Before contacting a service provider, gather the location, site type, access requirements, issue description and the result you need.

  • Site name and accurate location
  • Short description of the issue or work
  • Access restrictions and site contact
  • Deadline, urgency and preferred callback
◎
Checklist 02

Environmental issue: first information

For a site concern, record observable facts while following the relevant site procedure and prioritising people's safety.

  • What was observed and when
  • Potential impact and current hazards
  • Actions already taken by the site team
  • Who has been notified

If there is immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency services and site response team; do not wait for a website reply.

▤
Checklist 03

Planning multi-site work

When coordinating more than one site, a consistent information pack helps clarify priorities and dependencies.

  • List each location and its main requirement
  • Rank priorities and deadlines
  • Identify site contacts and access windows
  • Note dependencies or other contractors
