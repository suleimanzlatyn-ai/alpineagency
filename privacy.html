Contact form behaviour

The enquiry form uses your browser to prepare an email in your default email application. It does not directly transmit information to a website database. Your email application and provider handle the message when you choose to send it.

Information you choose to send

If you contact Alpine by email or telephone, information you share may be used to respond. Avoid sending passwords, identity documents, sensitive personal data or confidential site-security details in an initial message.

Third-party services

This site may load fonts from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash. Those services may process connection information when your browser requests assets.

Questions

For questions about an enquiry, contact ops@alpineagencies.co.ke. This general page should be reviewed against Alpine's actual privacy practices and applicable Kenyan data-protection obligations before being treated as a formal legal privacy notice.
