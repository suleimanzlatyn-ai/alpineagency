Website enquiry information

What the form does

The enquiry form prepares a message using your device’s email application. It does not itself submit the form to a website database, and a message is not sent until you choose Send in your email application.

What you may enter

The form may include your name, organisation, email address, phone number, site location, topic and message. Share only information needed to explain your enquiry.

Do not send sensitive information

Do not include passwords, financial account details, identity document numbers, confidential technical drawings or sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry. Agree a suitable secure route before sharing restricted documents.

Third-party services

The website may load fonts and photographs from external providers. Those providers may process standard technical data when your browser requests their resources. The site owner should review the providers actually used and update this notice accordingly.

Questions or requests

For questions about the handling of an enquiry, contact Alpine using the contact details on this website. This draft is not a substitute for review against applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements and Alpine’s real practices.
