Maintenance framework

Four questions worth answering.

Use these themes to prepare a useful brief and help establish the right next step.

01 / ASSET⌁

What is the asset?

Identify the equipment or system, location, age if known, operating conditions and how it supports the site.

02 / CONDITION◈

What is known?

Summarise symptoms, inspection records, previous repairs, manufacturer guidance and any reports already available.

03 / PRIORITY↗

What is the consequence?

Explain safety implications, operational disruption, environmental exposure and any time-critical constraints.

04 / ACTION✓

What needs confirming?

Clarify scope, access, competence, parts or specialist support, responsibilities and how completion will be recorded.
