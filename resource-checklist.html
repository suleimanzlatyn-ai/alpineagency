Site Readiness Checklist

A structured checklist to help prepare for a conversation about an environmental, engineering or infrastructure issue.

Discuss your requirement ↗Back to insights
A practical starting point

Turn observations into clear actions.

General information only. This guide is not a substitute for site-specific assessment, emergency advice, engineering design or qualified environmental and legal guidance.

SECTION 01

01 — Define the issue

Describe the site, asset or activity involved. Explain what changed, when it was noticed, and the outcome you need.

SECTION 02

02 — Record location and constraints

Note the site location, access requirements, operating hours, permits, nearby receptors and any restrictions.

SECTION 03

03 — Gather existing information

Collect drawings, inspection records, maintenance logs, permits, photographs and prior reports where available.

SECTION 04

04 — Consider safety and urgency

Identify hazards, isolation needs, exposure risks and whether specialist response is required. Never delay urgent action to complete a checklist.

SECTION 05

05 — Confirm responsibilities

Identify the site owner, decision-maker, contact person and who can approve access or work.

SECTION 06

06 — Agree next steps

Clarify scope, deliverables, qualifications, schedule, exclusions and communication.
