Context first · Responsible choices
Working principles

A professional standard starts with clarity.

The following principles shape the intended experience and enquiry process.

01 / CLARITY

Define the problem first

Understand the location, the operating context and the desired outcome before discussing solutions.

02 / RESPONSIBILITY

Put safety and environment in view

Recognise immediate hazards, compliance requirements and the need for suitably qualified specialists.

03 / PRACTICALITY

Focus on feasible next steps

Consider timing, access, asset condition, available information and realistic delivery constraints.

04 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Make responsibilities explicit

Confirm scope, decision-makers, communication, deliverables and follow-up actions.
