Frequently asked

Useful details. No guesswork.

Alpine should confirm actual availability, qualifications, pricing and delivery arrangements for every specific request.

What information should I provide?

Include your organisation, site location, asset or system involved, a short description of the requirement and any relevant timing or safety constraints.

Can Alpine cover every service listed on the website?

Not necessarily. These pages describe areas for discussion. Confirm current service availability, qualifications, delivery arrangements and specialist partners directly before relying on a specific capability.

Can I request support outside Nairobi?

Share the exact site location and timing. Geographic coverage, mobilisation and partner availability must be confirmed for the specific request.

What if the situation is an emergency?

If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services and follow your site's emergency plan first. This website is not an emergency dispatch service.

Does the website form send my information automatically?

The current form prepares an email in your email application. You must review and send that email to complete your enquiry; the website does not submit the form to a database.

How do I become a supplier or contractor?

Use the procurement page to describe your organisation, relevant capability, operating area and any verified credentials or documentation available for review.

Can I request a site visit or quotation?

You can start a conversation through the contact or consultation page. Any visit, scope, price and timing must be confirmed directly by Alpine.
