The context

Health, Safety & Site Readiness

Health and safety requirements depend on the site, work activity and applicable Kenyan laws and standards. Specialist reviews and statutory duties must be handled by competent, authorised people.

01 — Site and contractor coordination

Clarify access arrangements, work boundaries, inductions, permits and communication between site stakeholders.

02 — Risk assessment and method planning

Identify hazards and establish the need for task-specific risk assessments, method statements and competent supervision.

03 — Emergency arrangements

Confirm site emergency procedures, contact routes, assembly arrangements and coordination with local emergency services.

04 — Training and competency requirements

Identify role-specific training and verify the qualifications required for each task before work starts.

Define the right scope

Requirements vary by site, asset, urgency and applicable standards. Confirm the actual service scope, technical qualifications, insurance, delivery capacity, price and schedule directly with Alpine Agencies before appointment.
