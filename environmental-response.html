Environmental response

Environmental or site incidents need clear information, safe decisions and prompt communication. Use this page to start a response-coordination enquiry.

First priorities

Make the situation clear. Keep people safe.

If a site issue has environmental or operational impact, share the location, what happened, whether people or the environment may be at risk, and what action has already been taken.

  • Identify immediate risks to people and the surrounding environment.
  • Follow site emergency procedures and notify the responsible site lead.
  • Contact the relevant emergency or regulatory authority when required.
  • Share clear facts, not assumptions, when requesting further support.
If there is immediate danger, injury, fire, a hazardous release or another emergency, do not wait for a website reply. Move to safety and contact the appropriate emergency services and site response team.
◎

Information to include

Where?

Site location, nearest landmark and safe access details.

What happened?

Describe the observed issue, when it began and what is known.

Current risk

People affected, visible release, access hazard or environmental concern.

Who is coordinating?

Site lead, callback number and actions already taken.

Response coordination

Clarity before assumptions.

Each incident has its own risks and obligations. Response availability, scope, required credentials and timing must be confirmed directly.

Report facts

Use concise observations and distinguish confirmed information from estimates.

Follow site procedure

Respect site-specific emergency, safety, environmental and reporting protocols.

Confirm responsibilities

Ensure site management and relevant authorities know who is doing what.
