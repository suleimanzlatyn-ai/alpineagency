Readiness is a system

Make responsibilities and escalation routes clear.

Spills, suspected pollution, flooding or equipment failure can affect people, the environment and operations. Preparedness starts with clear contacts, site information, safe isolation procedures and access to qualified responders.

  • Document site address, access and emergency contacts
  • Identify sensitive drains, waterways and other receptors
  • Keep relevant safety data and site plans accessible
  • Define who can authorise specialist contractors and reporting
Prepare · Escalate · Record
If an incident is happening now

Prioritise people and immediate safety.

This is general planning guidance, not an emergency response service or substitute for local authorities and qualified responders.

01

Protect people

Follow the site emergency plan, move away from danger and contact local emergency services where there is immediate risk.

02

Prevent escalation safely

Only trained and authorised personnel should attempt isolation or containment, and only when safe.

03

Escalate promptly

Notify the responsible site manager and relevant authorities according to incident type and requirements.

04

Record known facts

From a safe location, note the time, location, material if known and affected area.

05

Protect waterways

Do not enter drains, water or contaminated areas. Tell responders about nearby drains and watercourses.

06

Arrange specialist help

Use qualified response professionals appropriate to the material, risk and local requirements.
