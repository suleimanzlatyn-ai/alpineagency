A practical framework for prioritising maintenance around consequence, condition and operating need.

01

Understand the consequence

Consider safety, environmental, service, financial and reputational impacts if the asset fails.

02

Use condition evidence

Review inspection results, age, operating history, recurring defects and manufacturer guidance.

03

Prioritise proportionately

Define inspection and maintenance intervals with qualified input and applicable standards, rather than relying on one schedule for every asset.

04

Learn from the work

Record failures, corrective actions, repeat issues and changes in risk so plans can improve over time.
