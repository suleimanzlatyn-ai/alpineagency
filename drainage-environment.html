Our working principles

Water pathways are part of site risk

Use this as a guide to the first conversation. Any technical assessment, emergency service or regulated work must be confirmed with qualified providers.

STEP 01

Listen and understand

What is happening? Where is the site? What outcome is needed and by when?

STEP 02

Clarify the context

Discuss access, urgency, existing information, constraints and safety considerations.

STEP 03

Define the scope

Agree the task, deliverables, responsibilities, specialist needs and commercial basis.

STEP 04

Confirm and communicate

Confirm feasibility and keep the next action and expectations clear.

Before we start

Useful information makes the first conversation better.

When possible, prepare the site location, a short description of the issue, timing, relevant photographs, asset information and any known safety or access constraints.

What to expect
