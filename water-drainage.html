Follow the water. Understand the risk.

Drainage, wastewater, stormwater and containment issues can create operational disruption and environmental exposure. Understanding where water comes from, where it flows and what it may carry is a useful first step.

Capability overview

Water, Drainage & Pollution Control

A structured water and drainage enquiry should establish the system, the location of the concern, the weather or operating conditions involved, and whether immediate safety or environmental risks are present.

01 / CAPABILITY

Drainage & stormwater

Describe blockages, flooding, runoff routes, discharge points and recurring problems during heavy rain.

02 / CAPABILITY

Wastewater & water quality

Clarify the source, suspected issue, sampling or monitoring already completed and specialist advice needed.

03 / CAPABILITY

Containment & pollution pathways

Review the concern around separators, bunds, storage areas, drains and pathways to soil or surface water.

04 / CAPABILITY

Prevention & maintenance

Discuss inspection routines, maintenance needs and practical steps to reduce repeat incidents.

What to include in an enquiry

If there is an active spill or immediate danger, keep people safe and contact the appropriate emergency or specialist response service first.

  • Site or facility location
  • Asset, system or concern involved
  • Timing and urgency
  • Known constraints or reports
