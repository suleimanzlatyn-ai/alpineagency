Capability overview

Engineering & Planned Maintenance

Reactive work can disrupt operations. A structured view of asset criticality, observed condition and maintenance history helps teams plan the right next action.

Actual service scope, qualifications, availability and delivery arrangements should be confirmed directly before engagement. Where specialist work is needed, roles and responsibilities should be agreed before work begins.

01 / FOCUS AREA

Maintenance planning

Organise assets, tasks, frequency, responsibilities and records around operating needs.

02 / FOCUS AREA

Fault identification & escalation

Capture symptoms and site context, identify immediate risks and refer technical questions to competent professionals.

03 / FOCUS AREA

Work coordination

Consider access, permits, isolation, contractors, safety controls, timing and communication with site teams.

04 / FOCUS AREA

Close-out & learning

Record work completed, outstanding issues and recommended follow-up.
