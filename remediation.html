Capability overview

Contaminated Land & Remediation

Land quality concerns require a site-specific, evidence-led approach. The right sequence depends on site history, intended use, potential receptors and competent assessment findings.

Actual service scope, qualifications, availability and delivery arrangements should be confirmed directly before engagement. Where specialist work is needed, roles and responsibilities should be agreed before work begins.

01 / FOCUS AREA

Site history & preliminary review

Gather land-use records, site plans, incident history and information about potentially contaminating activities.

02 / FOCUS AREA

Investigation planning

Define the questions to answer, likely contaminants, sampling strategy and specialist qualifications needed.

03 / FOCUS AREA

Risk & remediation options

Evaluate findings, exposure pathways, intended land use, regulatory context and feasible risk-management options.

04 / FOCUS AREA

Verification & follow-up

Plan documentation, validation, monitoring and ongoing controls where required.
