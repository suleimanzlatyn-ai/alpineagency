Areas to consider

Start with the questions that matter.

These are discussion areas, not a claim that every technical service is provided directly by Alpine. Confirm scope and qualified delivery arrangements before work begins.

FOCUS / 01◈

Site history & preliminary review

Gather available land-use records, site plans, incident history and information about potentially contaminating activities.

FOCUS / 02⌁

Investigation planning

Define the questions to answer, likely contaminants, sampling strategy and specialist qualifications needed.

FOCUS / 03≈

Risk & remediation options

Evaluate findings, exposure pathways, intended land use, regulatory context and feasible risk-management options.

FOCUS / 04↗

Verification & follow-up

Plan documentation, validation, monitoring and ongoing controls where required by the approved remediation approach.
