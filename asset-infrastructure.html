Service overview

Lifecycle considerations

Asset-related requirements may include condition reviews, inspection planning, maintenance priorities, upgrade coordination, operational interfaces and end-of-life considerations.

What to prepare

Asset type and location, known faults, inspection and maintenance history, operating criticality, drawings and constraints on access or downtime.

Possible next steps

Define the asset scope, identify required technical expertise, agree inspection or maintenance boundaries and document responsibilities for follow-up work.
