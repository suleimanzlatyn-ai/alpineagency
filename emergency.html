Response enquiries

What to share when you call.

  • Exact site location and a safe contact person.
  • Incident type: fuel, oil, chemical, drainage or equipment.
  • When it started and whether the situation is ongoing.
  • Any known hazards, access restrictions or immediate safety actions already taken.

Do not enter a hazardous area or attempt to handle unknown substances. Follow your site emergency plan and contact local emergency services where there is an immediate threat to life or safety.

Common questions

Before you contact us.

What information should I prepare?

Prepare the location, a description of the issue, any known hazards, the affected equipment or substance, and your contact details.

Can I request planned work rather than emergency support?

Yes. Use the contact form to discuss audits, tank and fuel infrastructure work, drainage maintenance, inspection or preventative maintenance.

Is a response time guaranteed?

No fixed response time is promised on this page. Availability depends on the location, nature of the incident and team capacity.
