Climate Resilience Review

A structured starting point for considering flood, heat, water, ecological and continuity risks at a site.

Discuss your requirement ↗Back to insights
A practical starting point

Turn observations into clear actions.

General information only. This guide is not a substitute for site-specific assessment, emergency advice, engineering design or qualified environmental and legal guidance.

SECTION 01

Identify climate-related hazards

Consider flooding, intense rainfall, heat, drought, water stress, erosion and other locally relevant hazards.

SECTION 02

Map critical assets and dependencies

Identify critical equipment, access routes, utilities, suppliers and activities that could be affected.

SECTION 03

Review exposure and vulnerability

Consider asset condition, drainage capacity, shading, backup arrangements and the consequences of service interruption.

SECTION 04

Explore adaptation options

Consider maintenance, drainage improvements, nature-based measures, operational changes and resilient design.

SECTION 05

Prioritise feasible actions

Compare urgency, risk reduction, cost, maintenance burden and co-benefits before selecting measures.

SECTION 06

Monitor and refresh

Update the review after incidents, site changes, new information or changes in operating conditions.
