Environmental Risk Review

A practical framework for identifying environmental pathways, prioritising concerns and preparing a clear site review.

Discuss your requirement ↗Back to insights
A practical starting point

Turn observations into clear actions.

General information only. This guide is not a substitute for site-specific assessment, emergency advice, engineering design or qualified environmental and legal guidance.

SECTION 01

Identify activities and hazards

List activities, materials, storage areas and conditions that could create environmental risk.

SECTION 02

Map pathways and receptors

Consider drains, surface water, groundwater, soil, air, nearby communities and sensitive habitats.

SECTION 03

Review existing controls

Check containment, drainage protection, inspection routines, spill equipment, signage and contractor controls.

SECTION 04

Prioritise risk

Document what could happen, likelihood, possible impact and which controls reduce exposure.

SECTION 05

Record evidence and actions

Use dated observations, appropriate photos and an action log with owners and target dates.

SECTION 06

Confirm compliance needs

Seek qualified advice on applicable permits, reporting duties and regulatory requirements for the specific site.
