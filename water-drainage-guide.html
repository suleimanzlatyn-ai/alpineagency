Water & Drainage Checklist

A practical review list for drainage pathways, water-related risks and pollution prevention at operational sites.

Discuss your requirement ↗Back to insights
A practical starting point

Turn observations into clear actions.

General information only. This guide is not a substitute for site-specific assessment, emergency advice, engineering design or qualified environmental and legal guidance.

SECTION 01

Understand the drainage layout

Locate surface-water drains, foul drains, interceptors, discharge points and any sensitive receiving environments.

SECTION 02

Inspect condition and flow

Look for blockages, unusual odours, staining, ponding, damaged covers or evidence of uncontrolled discharge.

SECTION 03

Review spill pathways

Understand where a spill could travel and whether drains or containment areas need suitable protection.

SECTION 04

Check maintenance records

Review inspection dates, cleaning records, faults, repairs and any required testing.

SECTION 05

Confirm discharge requirements

Verify permits, consents, sampling needs and applicable limits with qualified specialists or authorities.

SECTION 06

Record and escalate concerns

Photograph observations safely, record locations and escalate suspected pollution or immediate hazards promptly.
