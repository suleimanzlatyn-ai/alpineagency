What to include in your RFQ or tender enquiry

Provide the details that are available. We will clarify any gaps directly with you.

01 / SCOPE

Scope of work

Describe the service, asset, issue or deliverable you need.

02 / SITE

Location & access

Include the site location, access restrictions and any safety induction requirements.

03 / TIMING

Deadlines & schedule

Share tender closing dates, site visit windows and expected delivery timelines.

04 / DOCUMENTS

Specifications

Attach relevant scope documents, asset schedules, drawings or technical requirements.

05 / COMPLIANCE

Required evidence

List the registrations, standards, insurance or supporting documents requested in the tender.

06 / RESPONSE

Submission instructions

Include the required format, procurement contact and submission channel.
