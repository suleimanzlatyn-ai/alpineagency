Asset lifecycle thinking

Plan before an asset becomes an urgent problem.

Ageing equipment, incomplete condition records and deferred maintenance can create operational, safety and environmental concerns. Start by understanding the asset, its role, condition and consequence of failure.

  • Clarify asset type, age, criticality and service history
  • Identify inspection and maintenance records
  • Prioritise risks and operational dependencies
  • Plan upgrades, replacement or decommissioning requirements
