Practical planning points for safer escalation and response coordination.

01

Know who to contact

Keep emergency contacts, site responsible persons and qualified specialist responders current and accessible.

02

Understand site layout

Maintain accurate site plans, drain locations, access points and relevant hazard information where appropriate.

03

Check readiness

Review procedures, training, suitable equipment and escalation routes. Equipment must match the likely hazard and be maintained.

04

After the event

Follow applicable reporting rules, document actions and arrange appropriate professional assessment and remediation.
