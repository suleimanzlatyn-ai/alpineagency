What a useful case study contains

From the challenge to the measurable outcome.

When approved project material is available, each story should follow a consistent structure that is useful to prospective clients and procurement teams.

01 / CONTEXT⌕

The challenge

Describe the site, operating environment, constraint and the decision that needed to be made.

02 / SCOPE⌁

The agreed role

State what Alpine delivered directly, what other specialists did and which responsibilities sat with the client.

03 / DELIVERY↗

The approach

Explain the process, coordination, constraints and key decisions without exposing confidential details.

04 / EVIDENCE✓

The outcome

Use verifiable outcomes, accepted deliverables or documented improvements—not unsupported claims.

05 / LEARNING✳

Lessons & next steps

Show relevant maintenance implications, follow-up actions or practical lessons learned.

06 / APPROVAL◈

Approved materials

Include only authorised client names, logos, photographs, quotations and site details.

How we frame a project

Clear expectations before mobilisation.

A credible project begins with a defined scope, appropriate expertise, realistic timing and an agreed communication route. The approach depends on the actual requirement and site context.

  • Understand the issue and intended result
  • Confirm scope, exclusions and responsibilities
  • Identify site access, safety and technical constraints
  • Agree acceptance criteria and close-out records
