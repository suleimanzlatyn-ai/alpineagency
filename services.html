Our services

Specialist support for environmental and infrastructure assets.

Browse the service areas below. Availability and the exact scope depend on site conditions, equipment and project requirements; contact us to discuss your specific needs.

EnvironmentalFuel infrastructureDrainage & waterInspection & maintenanceLand & waste
01

Environmental consultancy & compliance

Environmental site audits, risk assessment, contamination assessment and ESG-related reporting support. Scope is agreed around the site, applicable requirements and intended use of the report.

02

Spill & incident response

Enquiries for fuel, oil and chemical spill response, containment, recovery and post-incident documentation. For urgent incidents, call the team and confirm the location, substance and immediate hazards.

03

Fuel & energy infrastructure

Support for fuel storage and transfer systems, tank integrity checks, fuel uplift and polishing, infrastructure testing, tank cleaning, coating and lining, and road tanker testing where applicable.

04

Drainage, separators & bunds

Drainage surveys and maintenance, jetting and repair, oil-water separator installation or servicing, and secondary containment inspection and repair.

05

Inspection, testing & planned maintenance

Non-destructive testing enquiries, pipework and tank inspection, hazardous-area electrical testing, and planned preventative maintenance scheduling.

06

Land, waste & ecological support

Contaminated land assessment and remediation support, hazardous waste handling coordination, and ecology or fisheries service enquiries where project needs require them.

07

Decommissioning & transition planning

Discuss safe decommissioning of legacy fuel assets and upgrade planning for changing infrastructure requirements.
