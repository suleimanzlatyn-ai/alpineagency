Energy infrastructure

Infrastructure for today. Planning for tomorrow.

Energy and fuel-related sites must balance reliability, environmental protection, safety, maintenance and evolving infrastructure requirements.

Discuss your requirement ↗All services

Bring asset condition and transition plans together.

Energy infrastructure planning may involve existing asset condition, maintenance records, storage and containment considerations, upgrade options and future transition requirements. Confirm the specific services Alpine can provide before relying on this page as a statement of capability.

Fuel systems and electrical infrastructure require competent specialists and appropriate permits, procedures and safety controls.

Questions to consider

  • Which assets are critical to site operations?
  • What inspections, maintenance or compliance checks are due?
  • Are there known leaks, containment, drainage or equipment concerns?
  • What access, shutdown, safety and authorisation controls apply?
  • What future upgrades or lower-impact options are being considered?

Formal assessments, regulated work, specialist engineering and emergency services should only be undertaken by suitably qualified and authorised professionals. Confirm Alpine’s specific scope and qualifications before engagement.
