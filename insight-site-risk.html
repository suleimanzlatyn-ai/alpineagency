Environmental risk: source, pathway and receptor

A practical way to organise early environmental risk thinking before commissioning a site-specific assessment.

01

Source

Identify the activity, material, asset or condition that could create an impact. Consider normal operations and foreseeable failures.

02

Pathway

Consider how the impact might move: through drains, soil, groundwater, air, surface water or direct contact.

03

Receptor

Identify who or what could be affected, including workers, neighbours, sensitive habitats, water bodies and site assets.

04

Controls and evidence

Record existing controls, known gaps, monitoring data, incidents and actions. A qualified specialist can determine what assessment is appropriate.
