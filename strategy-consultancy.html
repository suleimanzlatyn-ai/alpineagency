Why this matters

Good decisions begin with the right questions.

Organisations may need to understand site conditions, pollution pathways, permit obligations, ecological constraints or sustainability priorities. The appropriate assessment depends on the site and the decision being made.

  • Define the decision or operational issue
  • Review existing reports, monitoring data and site records
  • Identify risks, receptors and information gaps
  • Determine whether accredited or specialist assessment is required
